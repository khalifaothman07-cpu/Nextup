Questions

Before you ask.

What does "backing" an artist actually get me?
A stake tied to that artist's career as a whole — not one release. As their catalogue and audience grow, your position is tied to that growth. You also show up on their page as an early backer.
How is owning a song different from backing an artist?
Backing is tied to the artist's whole future. Owning a song is tied to one specific track — it's yours permanently, tradeable immediately, whether or not the artist ever releases anything else.
Is this investment advice?
No. Backing an artist or owning a song carries real financial risk, including possible loss of your full contribution. Nothing on this site is investment advice or a solicitation.
Where is Nextup available?
We're rolling out first in Bahrain and the wider GCC, with limited spots during early access. Availability elsewhere is confirmed during onboarding.
How do I get on the platform as an artist?
Artist onboarding isn't open yet — join the waitlist and note that you're an artist when we reach out.
